Lifecycle coordinate caution
Entry reanchors raw neutral top-left to occupied composite origin: Medium(+8,+6),
Light(+11,+7), Heavy(+6,+4), all native pixels. Heavy includes its special(+6,+2)
shift on top of the template(+0,+2) shift. Unload assembly00458b86..00458b8b
has an additional SUB EDI,4 for tank families after the literal unload table.
This instruction was omitted by the saved decompiler expression; ignoring it
would incorrectly suggest4px drift per boarding cycle. Full tank exits therefore
use Medium(-8,-6),Light(-11,-7),Heavy(-6,-4), exact inverses of entry. Do not
change renderer anchors to hide a lifecycle error.
Fresh lifecycle caller audit confirms ordinary tank boarding goes through
00450db0->00450cd0 and sets both hull and turret direction6, west (browser PI).
The special preserved-direction path00456c90 is reached for APC descriptor
sprite5173:00450890 sets its special unit+0x8a bit0x10 and object+0x21 direction.
Special exit00459720 writes direction*5 to object+0x21. This is not the ordinary
Light/Medium/Heavy exit sequence. Their parked sprite IDs remain the static
template8/26/29 frames, not a run of8 directional frames.
Crew death also converts usable hardware through00458ec0->00458af0. The browser
applies the same occupied-to-parked origin conversion on this transition, once
even if several bullets kill the crew in the same tick. A dead crew does not
spawn a living robot or change tank type or surviving hull health.

Rendering bounds
Occupied tanks are composite images. Selection/hit bounds and HD adapters must
cover the union of the actual hull/turret/barrel/hatch rectangles. A generic
radius or one neutral sprite rectangle cannot represent those dimensions.
HD tanks now measure the visible alpha envelope once when preparing each cached
pose, with an edge guard, instead of shrinking into a fixed90x72 whitespace crop.
Their occupied turret uses the faction palette. Empty HD tanks use one fixed
west-facing gray pose regardless of stale body/aim angles, matching the static
native parked-template behavior. This is newly drawn HD art, not original art
at higher resolution; native-bound fitting remains a browser presentation choice.
