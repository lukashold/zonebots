INDEPENDENT BEHAVIOR SPECIFICATION — Z (1996)
Research date: 2026-10-30. Fresh prose from public primary publications only. No local prior game source, executable, installed data, reverse-engineering output, or prior project documents were read. No original implementation, art, or map imagery is copied.

CONFIRMED RULES — implementation priority P0: territory/production, robot crews/combat, match result; P1: services and world traversal.
M, printed p3:
- Touch a territory flag with a friendly unit to transfer the territory immediately. Buildings there follow territory ownership; flags can be recaptured.
- Territorial gains increase factory throughput, vehicle-repair speed, and radar coverage. Losses reverse these advantages.
- Factories and forts allow product selection. Factories replenish robots/vehicles; repair buildings mend vehicles; radar reveals enemy positions.
- Robots form controllable units: selecting any member selects the unit. Robots can operate vehicles and guns. Contextual orders include movement and attack.
- Entering the opposing fort or eliminating opposing forces wins; protect your own fort and forces.
- The campaign has five planets, four battles each.

G, printed pp2-4:
- Capturing a nearly finished factory can appropriate its output; changing its product before capture denies that advantage.
- Robots sprint toward flags/hardware without returning fire. Movement ordered under attack accelerates units and suppresses return fire.
- Roads accelerate vehicles. Destroying occupied bridges kills crossing units. Tanks/guns can fire over buildings.
G, pp4,6-8:
- Robots occupy neutral hardware; squad members remaining after boarding continue separately. Sniping drivers permits chassis theft.
- Grunts distract; Psychos threaten jeep drivers; Toughs counter Snipers; Snipers/Lasers threaten crews; Pyros excel against slow armor/structures. Tough/Pyro APC combinations are effective.
G, pp7,8,11,18:
- Demonstrated permissions: 1-star vehicle factory: Light Tank; 2-star robot factory: Psycho; 2-star vehicle factory: Medium Tank; 5-star robot factory: Laser.
G, pp7,8,10,18,19:
- Factory damage slows production. Robots cross water; lava constrains routes; chasms block every unit; cranes restore ruined buildings/bridges.
G, p2:
- Single-player layouts give both halves matching resources; the opponent reacts to player decisions.
G, pp6-7:
- Robots can collect grenades and breach destructible walls/rocks while executing travel orders.
G, pp3,13:
- Artillery can bombard forts from outside defensive gun range.

UNVERIFIED DETAILS AND ESTIMATE POLICY
Accessible primary evidence does not establish exact squad sizes by robot type, grenade capacities, APC capacity/unload controls/timing, crew selection order, the complete factory-star eligibility matrix, campaign technology gates, fort-destruction termination semantics, radar geometry/fog rules, self-repair, driver-death probabilities, or exact pathfinding/target priorities.

The manual contents point to manufacturing on pp11-12, robots/grenades/vehicles on p13, artillery and winning on p14. These are research leads, not inspected evidence. Do not present an APC unloading convention or full star table as verified original behavior.

Use tunable, explicitly labeled ESTIMATE tables for all unsourced numerical balance: production duration/scaling, factory damage penalty, health, damage, splash, range, reload, movement/road multipliers, repair speed, radar radius, capture contact radius and crew-hit probability. The observed permissions are examples, not a complete eligibility matrix. Any other eligibility is provisional. Do not claim numerical fidelity to the executable.

Engineering assumptions recommended for a new implementation, rather than claims about the original: use persistent entity identities; keep empty hardware separate from its crew; conserve robot count and health across crew changes and APC load/unload; make selectable orders and production state inspectable; keep terminal match states explicit; put world movement constraints in simulation data. These policies make behavior reviewable while leaving original numerical uncertainties visible.
