Fort banners and guest color perspective

The original places32x24 banners on both fort roofs, at nativebase+(83,27).
The browser doubles these coordinates/dimensions. Their normal presence is
confirmed for all20 campaign imports; ordinary territory flags at fort-sector
positions are correctly excluded by the initialized native fort flags.

The two fort-banner banks contain baked red16..31 and blue32..47 pixels.
Guest perspective now swaps those two index bands only for genuine fortBanner
records. It preserves the sprite,shape,alpha,position and existing palette
state. The banks cannot simply be exchanged:several frames have different
alpha/opaque pixels. Host records,shadows,ordinary flags,white/neutral/literal
palettes and following inherited effects keep their existing paths.

The marker is local derived guest metadata,never a host wire field. Tests use
four actual canonical native frames,all five palette worlds and actual guest/
Art submissions,including following250/557 inherited effects. This validates
the browser perspective policy; it is not full Original multiplayer-startup
or GPU raster equivalence. Original sprite pixels remain private assets.
